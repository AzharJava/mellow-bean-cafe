The beginning

Slow coffee. Bright mornings.

We opened Mellow Bean for the people who believe a good day can start with a good cup of coffee. Our space is made for pauses: the first page of a book, a catch-up that runs long, or five quiet minutes before the world gets busy.

“The best part of a café is what happens between the sips.”

What we believe

Small details. Big feeling.

We care about the ingredients, the people, and the feeling you take with you when you leave.

✦

Made with care
